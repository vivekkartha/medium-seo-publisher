- Added composable modes for SEO creation, editorial-only creation, prose revision, title refresh, and publication.

- Replaced the misleading installation discovery check with installed-skill verification.

## Remaining evidence limits

Live provider retrieval, live Medium editing, and a real ambiguous network submission were not executed in this review. Those require authorized accounts and a controllable browser. Cases not listed above remain proposed tests. Treat the results as evidence that the instructions contain the required decision paths, not as proof that every host or changing website UI will execute them correctly.
