# Article source protocol

Keyword metrics show demand and competition; they do not verify the article's factual claims.

For each material claim, retain a compact record with the claim, source, publication or retrieval date, and whether the source directly supports it. Prefer primary or official sources for product behavior, platform policy, research, statistics, and other mutable facts. Use current sources when the fact can change. Cite the supporting page near the claim when the article format allows it.

Treat the repository and its committed files as primary evidence for what this skill instructs. Package validation proves only package structure. Designed evaluation cases, model self-assessments, installs, and stars do not prove accuracy, ranking, conversion, or successful real-world use.

User-supplied experience can support personal observations when it is clearly attributed to the author. Never invent a test, outcome, quotation, personal reaction, or first-person event. If a material claim cannot be verified, remove it, narrow it, or label the uncertainty plainly.

Before delivery, check that every citation still points to the intended source and that edits have not separated a claim from its qualifier or evidence.
