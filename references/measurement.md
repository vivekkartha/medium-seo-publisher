# Outcomes and measurement

Choose one primary outcome before research. For Medium, reasonable goals include engaged reads, followers, or member reading time. Signups require an actual destination and trackable path. Do not treat claps as equivalent to revenue or followers.

Capture available baseline data and its window. Separate search referrals from Medium distribution when the account exposes them. Record inaccessible metrics as unknown; do not imply access to Medium's infrastructure or a verified Search Console property.

Prepare reviews at 14, 30, 60, and 90 days, adapting to available volume and the user's requested horizon. A written schedule is not an active automation. Set reminders or recurring jobs only on explicit request.

Record the published URL, version/date, changes, primary metric, observation window, and attribution limits. Compare with a suitable baseline or comparable articles when possible. A before/after difference alone does not prove the edit caused it.

Diagnose in order: discoverability; visibility-to-click performance; reading engagement; then conversion. Change one major editorial variable at a time where practical. Do not repeatedly change a headline before enough evidence accumulates.

Report observed outcomes and uncertainty. No skill can guarantee conversion, ranking, earnings, or GitHub stars.
